# Compatibility symbol sources

These shapes are used only to render matching private-use characters contained
in user messages. They are not micaGO branding.

- `apple.svg`: Simple Icons v16, Apple icon, CC0 1.0.
  <https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16/icons/apple.svg>
- `xiaomi.svg`: Simple Icons v16, Xiaomi icon, CC0 1.0.
  <https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16/icons/xiaomi.svg>
- `twitter-2012.svg`: Simple Icons v10, Twitter icon, CC0 1.0.
  <https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@10/icons/twitter.svg>

Brand names and logos may also be protected by trademark law.
