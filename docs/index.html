What it does

Use your Mac as the bridge.

Install the Mac Companion, pair the client, and keep messaging through the accounts already configured on your Mac.

iMessage through your Mac

Read and send iMessage from your client through your own Mac.

SMS through Mac

Use SMS relay when your Mac and Messages setup support it.

Rich messaging support

Send media and use modern chat features such as replies, reactions, link previews, voice messages, and group chats where supported.

Local-first bridge

Connect over LAN first, with optional remote access through your own public URL.

Built for daily chats.

Privacy model

Your messages do not pass through a micaGO hosted message cloud.

micaGO is designed around your Mac. The companion app reads from your local Messages setup and serves the devices you pair.

Remote access is optional. If you use it, you bring your own tunnel or public URL and protect it with your token.

Push notifications are optional and can be self-hosted through your own Firebase setup.

Platform notes

micaGO's client is built with Flutter, so additional platforms may be possible. The Android build is the primary tested release right now. Other builds are experimental until they are tested and documented.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is micaGO made by Apple?

No. micaGO is an independent open source project and is not affiliated with Apple.

Do I need a Mac?

Yes. micaGO uses your Mac as the bridge for iMessage and SMS.

Are my messages stored in a micaGO cloud?

No. micaGO does not provide a hosted message cloud for your conversations.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes, if you configure a public URL or tunnel that points to your Mac Companion.

Is it stable?

micaGO is beta software. Expect some rough edges while the project moves toward a stable release.
