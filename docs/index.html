Is this for me?

Check before you download.

Many people expect micaGO to put iMessage on Android by itself. It can't. It only works with a Mac you already own.

Good fit: you have a Mac

It sits at home or at work, stays on, and is signed in to Messages. That Mac is what talks to Apple.

Good fit: you chat on Android or Windows

You want to answer iMessages from a phone, a tablet, or a PC without walking back to the Mac.

Not a fit: you have no Mac

micaGO can't create an iMessage account or reach Apple on its own. Without your own Mac it won't work.

Not a fit: your Mac is often off

Messages only move while the Mac is awake. When it sleeps, micaGO waits for it to wake up.

What you can do

Chat from another screen.

Your Mac sends each reply from your own Apple ID, so your friends see nothing new.

iMessage chats

Read and reply to your conversations, group chats included.

Photos, voice messages, and reactions

Send pictures, record voice messages, reply to a specific message, or react to one.

Text messages (SMS)

Green-bubble texts work too once your iPhone forwards them to the Mac. Turn on Text Message Forwarding in the iPhone settings.

Away from home (optional)

On the same Wi-Fi it works right away. To use it anywhere else, set up your own secure link to the Mac. Remote access guide.

How it works

Set it up once.

1

Set up your Mac

Install the micaGO Companion on the Mac that is signed in to Messages. When macOS asks, allow Full Disk Access so it can read your messages. Mac setup guide.

2

Connect your phone or PC

Install micaGO on your device. On Android, scan the QR code in the Companion. On Windows, paste the pairing code it shows. The same Wi-Fi makes the first connection easiest. Connection guide.

3

Start chatting

Your conversations show up. Leave the Mac on and messages keep coming in both directions.

Privacy

Your messages stay between your own devices.

You don't sign up for anything, and micaGO has no cloud. No server of ours stores or reads your chats.

Remote access is optional. If you set it up, the link leads to your own Mac and is locked with a key only your devices know. Remote access guide.

Push notifications are optional too. They go through Google's push service with a Firebase project you create yourself. Notifications guide.

FAQ

Common questions.

Can I get iMessage on Android without a Mac?

No. micaGO needs your own Mac signed in to Messages, and that Mac does the real work.

Does my Mac have to stay on?

Yes. Nothing is sent or received while it's asleep or off. Turn on Keep Awake in the Companion to stop it from sleeping.

Will my friends see a different number or name?

No. Your Mac sends every message from your own Apple ID.

Is micaGO made by Apple?

No. It's an independent open source project with no ties to Apple.

Does micaGO keep my messages in a cloud?

No. micaGO has no cloud, so your messages stay on your own devices.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes, after you set up your own secure link to the Mac. Read the remote access guide.

Is it stable?

It's beta software. It works for everyday use, though you may hit a few rough edges.
